Percentiles are computed against the full sample; for running time and heart rate, lower values score higher. The tier estimate comes from a linear model of standardized measurements (R² ≈ 0.83 on tier rank, in-sample) and is an association-based estimate, not a causal or professional-scouting judgment. Sport is not an input because, in this dataset, the same traits predicted performance in every sport. Data: college-aged athletes; findings may not generalize to elite populations.
